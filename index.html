Safety First

Electrical Safety Matters

Electrical hazards can arise suddenly. Following basic preventative guidelines helps protect family, workers, and property from structural damage or injury.

Avoid Overloaded Sockets

Do not plug multiple high-power appliances into a single extension strip.

Replace Damaged Wires

Frayed or cracked wiring presents a direct shock and fire risk—replace immediately.

Keep Equipment Dry

Never operate switches or electrical tools with wet hands or near standing water.

Do Not Ignore Burning Smells

Sparks or burning odors indicate overheating wire insulation. Isolate power promptly.

Use Qualified Personnel

Avoid unverified DIY fixes on main service lines; contact experienced electricians.
